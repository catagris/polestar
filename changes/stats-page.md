- Stats: a new page in the hub (/pole stats, the minimap button's menu and the
  guide window's menu) that counts what your character does: quests,
  experience, time played, gold, deaths and where you play, with a map of the
  paths you walked, drawn on one map of the world that shows each zone in
  more detail as you zoom in, and of your journeys: the flight paths you
  flew, the boats and zeppelins you rode, and your portals, hearths and tram
  rides. Kept on your computer only;
  Options > Discovery > Stats turns it off.
